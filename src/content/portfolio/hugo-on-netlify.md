This guide is part of a series covering framework integrations on the Netlify platform. It walks developers through configuring and deploying a Hugo site on Netlify, covering build settings, environment variables, and deployment options.

The framework guides series was designed to give developers a consistent, opinionated starting point for each major static site generator and framework supported by the platform.
