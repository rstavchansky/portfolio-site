This career matrix was developed for the technical writing team at Netlify to give individual contributors a clear, structured path for growth across skill areas and seniority levels.

The work involved auditing an existing career ladder, identifying gaps in the progression criteria, and rewriting the framework to provide more granular, actionable benchmarks at each level.
