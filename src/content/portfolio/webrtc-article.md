This article walks Wowza Streaming Engine users through the process of ingesting a non-WebRTC source stream — such as RTSP, SRT, or RTMP — and playing it back using WebRTC.

The content bridges a gap for users who needed to work with legacy stream sources in a modern WebRTC delivery workflow, with step-by-step guidance and configuration examples.
